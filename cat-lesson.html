💡 Top 10 Everyday Expressions from the Video

Here are 10 highly practical phrases used by Cat that you can immediately start using in your everyday English conversations:

1. Bridge the gap
To connect or reduce the difference between two distinct stages or ideas.
"Daily conversation practice helped me bridge the gap between knowing English grammar and actually speaking fluently."
2. Tackle a task / problem
To start dealing with or solving a challenging task with energy and focus.
"Let me grab a coffee first, and then I'll tackle the most difficult emails on my list."
3. Lighten the load
To make work, effort, or stress easier and less overwhelming to manage.
"Delegating some administrative tasks to the assistant really helped lighten my load this week."
4. Keep track of (something)
To stay informed or maintain continuous records about something over time.
"I use a mobile app to keep track of my monthly expenses and budget."
5. Get a feel for (something)
To develop an intuitive understanding or familiarity with a process or environment.
"After working in the new office for a few days, I finally got a feel for how the team operates."
6. Backwards
Doing or approaching something in the reverse or wrong order.
"Buying expensive furniture before measuring the living room space was completely backwards!"
7. Seasoned (professional / learner)
Having extensive experience, skill, and practice in a specific activity.
"As a seasoned traveler, she always packs light and arrives at the airport two hours early."
8. Daunting task
A job or challenge that appears intimidating, overwhelming, or difficult at first.
"Writing a 50-page dissertation seemed like a daunting task, but breaking it down by chapter made it manageable."
9. Build on (something)
To use existing progress or knowledge as a foundation for further achievement.
"Once you master basic vocabulary, you can build on that foundation by reading native articles."
10. All in one
Combining multiple useful features or tools together into a single place or product.
"This productivity platform gives you calendars, notes, and task management all in one place."
