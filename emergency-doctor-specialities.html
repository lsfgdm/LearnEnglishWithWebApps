Executive Summary

Presented by Dr. Jubbal from Med School Insiders, this video explores the diverse landscape of emergency medicine (EM) subspecialties and fellowships. While subspecialization is not mandatory, completing a fellowship helps physicians tailor their practice to specific interests and lifestyle preferences.

Key Subspecialties Highlighted:

  • EMS: Systems-level care & paramedic protocols.
  • Medical Toxicology: Diagnostic detective work on poisonings.
  • Pediatric EM: Specialized pediatric physiology & family communication.
  • Sports Medicine: Clinic schedules & team coverage.
  • Critical Care: ICU intensivist work, ventilators, & resuscitations.
  • Pain Medicine: Predictable clinical scheduling.
  • Undersea & Hyperbaric: Dive injuries & pressure conditions.
  • Hospice & Palliative: End-of-life care & symptom management.
  • Wilderness Medicine: Remote, resource-limited environments.
Plus niche paths: Ultrasound (POCUS), Addiction, Brain Injury, Disaster, Informatics, Aerospace, & Telemedicine.
