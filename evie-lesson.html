10 Useful Expressions Extracted from the Video

Enhance your everyday English with these authentic phrases used by Evie in the video:

1. Mix it up
Meaning: To change or vary the way you do something to keep it interesting.
"I usually study grammar, but today I decided to mix it up and practice listening instead."
2. Freaking cool
Meaning: Extremely cool or impressive (an informal emphasis).
"Reaching 100,000 subscribers in just four months is freaking cool!"
3. Come across
Meaning: To give a specific impression to others.
"She was nervous during her first speech, but in this video she comes across as relaxed and confident."
4. Take the piss (out of someone)
Meaning: (British Informal) To make fun of or tease someone good-naturedly.
"Brits often take the piss out of their close friends to build rapport."
5. Pick up on
Meaning: To notice, understand, or perceive subtle cues, jokes, or meanings.
"It takes time to pick up on cultural jokes when you move to a new country."
6. Know the drill
Meaning: To already know the procedure, rules, or what is expected.
"Watch reality TV with no subtitles; you already know the drill!"
7. Go through the roof
Meaning: To increase rapidly to a very high level.
"If you replace your daily habits with English habits, your fluency will go through the roof."
8. Pull off
Meaning: To succeed in doing something difficult or ambitious.
"I have big plans for the channel, but I'm not sure if I can pull them off yet."
9. Mean a lot (to someone)
Meaning: To be very valuable, important, or appreciated by someone.
"All of your comments and support really mean a lot to me."
10. Banter
Meaning: Playful, witty, and lighthearted teasing remarks exchanged between people.
"British humor relies heavily on dry sarcasm and friendly banter."
