Documentary Highlights

This documentary explores the global resurgence of digital video game piracy, arguing it is driven by aggressive corporate anti-consumer practices rather than changing consumer ethics.

  • Decline of Physical Ownership: $80 games containing only download codes, Ubisoft's push against ownership, and digital movie deletions.
  • Failure of DRM: Uncrackable systems like Denuvo get bypassed, while paying users suffer restrictive "always-online" requirements.
  • Escalating Piracy Efforts: PS5 console jailbreaking and Nintendo cracking down by taking down 401 emulator projects on GitHub.
  • Role of AI & Service Problem: AI rebuilding legacy games, reinforcing Gabe Newell's thesis that piracy is a service issue.
