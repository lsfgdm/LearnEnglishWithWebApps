10 Key Expressions from the Video

1. Around the corner
Traduction : En une touche déviée / Rapide et fluide
In football, playing a quick one-touch pass behind a defender. In everyday life, it means something happening very soon or very close by.
Everyday Example: "Don't worry, the local supermarket is just around the corner."
2. Hold-up play
Traduction : Jeu en pivot / Jeu dos au but
The ability of an attacker to retain the ball under pressure until teammates arrive. Metaphorically, holding your ground while waiting for support.
Everyday Example: "His excellent hold-up play under pressure gave the team time to reorganize."
3. Back post / Far post
Traduction : Au second poteau
The goalpost furthest from where the ball is being crossed or passed.
Everyday Example: "The cross was precise, and the winger was waiting at the back post to tap it in."
4. The cherry on the cake
Traduction : La cerise sur le gâteau
An additional benefit that makes an already good situation complete or outstanding.
Everyday Example: "We won the championship, and getting a bonus was just the cherry on the cake."
5. Knocking on the door
Traduction : Taper à la porte / Être tout près de réussir
Creating continuous pressure or approaching a major achievement.
Everyday Example: "She has been knocking on the door of a promotion for two years."
6. Put someone on the spot
Traduction : Mettre quelqu'un au pied du mur / Mettre dans l'embarras
To force someone to answer a difficult question unexpectedly.
Everyday Example: "The manager put me on the spot during the meeting by asking for exact numbers."
7. Take something for granted
Traduction : Prendre pour acquis
To fail to appreciate something because you are so accustomed to it.
Everyday Example: "We often take good health for granted until we get sick."
8. Right place at the right time
Traduction : Au bon endroit au bon moment
Positioned perfectly by instinct or luck to take advantage of an opportunity.
Everyday Example: "I met my new business partner just by being in the right place at the right time."
9. Off the mark
Traduction : Débloquer son compteur / Démarrer
To get started, score the first points, or make initial progress.
Everyday Example: "The sales team got off the mark with three deals on Monday."
10. Change up (one's game)
Traduction : Varier son jeu / Adapter sa stratégie
To vary tactics or methods to stay unpredictable and overcome obstacles.
Everyday Example: "If your current marketing plan isn't working, you need to change it up."
