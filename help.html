Song Overview & Meaning

Song Theme: Written primarily by John Lennon, Help! reflects the emotional stress and pressure caused by sudden international stardom. Beneath its upbeat tempo, Lennon looks back at a time when he was younger, independent, and self-assured, contrasting it with his present state of vulnerability and need for support.
