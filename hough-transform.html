Core Technical Highlights

The Hough transform is a cornerstone algorithm in computer vision used by autonomous vehicle systems to identify continuous straight lines and geometries in noisy pixel data.

  • Brute-Force Problem: Calculating all point-pair lines is computationally prohibitive and prone to failure from noise.
  • Parameter Space: Maps pixels into curves where co-linear points intersect at exact peak coordinates.
  • Accumulator Grid: A matrix of accumulator cells that collect votes to highlight legitimate shapes.
  • Polar Coordinates: Uses $(\rho, \theta)$ angle-distance coordinates to prevent vertical line infinite slope errors ($m=\infty$).
