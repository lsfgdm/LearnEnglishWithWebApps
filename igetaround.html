4. 10 Useful Everyday Expressions

1. To get around

To travel frequently, move from place to place.

"He manages to get around using public transport."

👉 Traduction : Se déplacer / Bouger

2. To get bugged

To get annoyed, bored, or frustrated.

"Stop making that noise, I'm getting bugged!"

👉 Traduction : Être agacé / En avoir marre

3. The same old strip

The main road or street in a town where people hang out.

"On Friday nights, teens drive down the strip."

👉 Traduction : La même grande rue habituelle

4. To be hip

To be cool, trendy, and stylish.

"She knows all the newest bands; she's really hip."

👉 Traduction : Être branché / Cool

5. To make good bread

To earn a good amount of money (slang).

"After his promotion, he's making real good bread."

👉 Traduction : Gagner du bon fric

6. To be a cool head

To stay calm and composed under pressure.

"During an emergency, Sarah remains a cool head."

👉 Traduction : Garder son sang-froid

7. To go steady

To date someone exclusively (vintage expression).

"Are Tom and Lisa going steady now?"

👉 Traduction : Être en relation sérieuse

8. My buddies and me

A friendly, informal way to refer to your friends.

"My buddies and me are planning a trip."

👉 Traduction : Mes potes et moi

9. Leave (someone) alone

To stop bothering or interrupting someone.

"Please leave me alone, I need to focus."

👉 Traduction : Laisser tranquille

10. I gotta

Informal contraction for "I have got to" (I must).

"I gotta go now, my bus is coming!"

👉 Traduction : Je dois / Il faut que je...
