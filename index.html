You Only Need 9 Verbs to Speak Fluent English

English with Astrojoc • Light Verbs Strategy

Overview & Strategy Summary

Achieving fluency in English does not require memorizing thousands of rare verbs. Instead, by mastering 9 key "light verbs", you can form structural foundations for the vast majority of daily conversations. This reduces mental load and allows for natural, fluid speaking.

The 9 Light Verbs by Category

1. Make & Do (02:44)

Make: Used for creating results or outcomes (e.g., make a decision).

Do: Used for activities, tasks, or actions (e.g., do research).

2. Take & Get (03:47)

Take: Expresses deliberate action.

Get: Expresses change of state or result (the most versatile light verb).

3. Have & Give (04:55)

Have: Expresses experience or possession.

Give: Marks interaction and contribution.

4. Put, Set & Go (05:30)

Put: Used to position or place.

Set: Used to establish conditions.

Go: Expresses movement or change.

Training Method: "Hear, Map, Fire" (07:57)

  • Hear: Spot these natural light-verb combinations in real conversations.
  • Map: Associate the combination directly with a scenario or context, avoiding direct translation.
  • Fire: Speak the phrase aloud to pre-load your muscle memory before you need it in real situations (e.g., meetings or discussions).

10 Daily Expressions from the Video

Here are key expressions inspired by light verbs and concepts from the video that you can immediately apply in everyday situations:

1. Make a decision
To come to a conclusion or choice after thinking.
"We need to make a decision on the project before Friday."
2. Do research
To gather information or investigate a topic.
"Before buying a new car, I always do some research online."
3. Get ready
To prepare yourself for an event or action (change of state).
"Give me ten minutes to get ready and we can leave."
4. Take action
To start doing something to achieve a goal or solve a problem.
"If we want to hit our targets, we need to take action immediately."
5. Have a look
To check, inspect, or examine something briefly.
"Could you have a look at my document and give me feedback?"
6. Give thought to
To consider or reflect on something carefully.
"I'll give some thought to your proposal and email you tomorrow."
7. Put pressure on
To attempt to force someone to do something.
"Don't put too much pressure on yourself before the exam."
8. Set boundaries
To establish clear limits for personal or professional relationships.
"It's healthy to set boundaries between work and home life."
9. Go wrong
To fail, make a mistake, or turn into a bad outcome.
"If anything goes wrong, don't hesitate to call customer support."
10. Lighten the cognitive load
To make mental processing easier by simplifying structural patterns.
"Using fixed phrases helps lighten the cognitive load when speaking live."
