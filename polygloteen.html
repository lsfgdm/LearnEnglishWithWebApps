Listening Strategy (65–70% Context)

Focus on grasping the overall context (around 65–70%) rather than panicking over every single word (0:31–0:42).

Input vs. Output Balance

Input (podcasts, movies, series, reading) feeds comprehension; Output (speaking, writing, exercises) builds active command.

Consistency, Endurance & Rest

Treat learning as a resistance race, not a sprint: short regular sessions (e.g., 20 mins 3x/week) and brain rest beat draining marathons.

Using AI Wisely

AI tools help build study plans, exercises, and exam prep, but they do not replace a human teacher for nuanced personalized feedback.
