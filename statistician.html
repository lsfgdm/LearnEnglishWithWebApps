Proof Checked Documentary Analysis

Retired Statistician Solved a Famous Problem in Microsoft Word. Experts Ignored It.

Explore the extraordinary true story of Thomas Royen, who cracked the 50-year-old Gaussian correlation inequality using elementary formulas and Microsoft Word, only to be overlooked by the academic elite.

Key Takeaways

  • The Challenge: Proving the Gaussian correlation inequality regarding the probability of darts landing in overlapping shapes (circle and rectangle).
  • The Breakthrough: Thomas Royen used a variable knob ('C') to show probability strictly increases from 0 to 1, proving the conjecture.
  • Academic Bias: Dismissed because it was typed in MS Word instead of LaTeX, published in an obscure journal, by a retired professor.
  • Recognition: Finally validated and republished in December 2015 by mathematicians Rafał Latała and Dariusz Matlak.
